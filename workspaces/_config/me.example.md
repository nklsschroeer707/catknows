# Your personal setup — copy to `me.md` and fill in

`me.md` is gitignored: your communities, your handles, your paths stay on
your machine. Workspaces read this file when a contract says
"see workspaces/_config/me.md". Leave out what you don't use.

```markdown
# me

## My communities
- main: <your-community-slug>
- test: <optional test community slug>

## Feedback thread (for write/collect-feedback)
- community: <slug>
- post title: <the thread your testers report into>

## Maintainer mention (for write/report-a-bug's Skool fallback)
- display name: <Your Name>
- user id: <your Skool userId — find it via get_member_profile raw=true>
- feedback category: <category name in your community>

## Vault (for vault-librarian and snapshots)
- path: <absolute path to your Skool vault>

## Watched communities (for snapshots / research)
- <slug 1>
- <slug 2>
```
