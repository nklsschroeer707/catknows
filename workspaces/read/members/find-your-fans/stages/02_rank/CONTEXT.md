# Stage 02 — build the ranking

## Inputs
- (working) `../01_pull/output/engagement.md`

## Process
1. Count per member across all posts: likes given, comments written.
2. Rank by total engagement (comments weigh double — writing beats tapping).
3. Write: Top-10 table (Name @handle · Comments · Likes · What they engage
   with), then two sentences on patterns worth knowing (e.g. "your top
   commenter never posts herself").

## Outputs
- `latest.md` → `output/`

## Completion
Done when the ranking exists and the owner could thank their top 3 fans by
name tomorrow morning.
