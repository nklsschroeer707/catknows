catknows

Your Skool community, answerable.

catknows connects your Skool community to your AI assistant. Ask about members, posts, comments, engagement — in plain language, against live data, without exporting a spreadsheet first.

claude — catknows connected
Who joined this month but never posted?

What it reads

Everything your own Skool account can already see, through Skool's own endpoints — the same requests the website makes when you click around.

  • membersNames, roles, levels, points, last active
  • postsContent, labels, comments, likes
  • profilesBios, links, membership history
  • metricsAdmin dashboard numbers, if you're an admin
  • calendarEvents and classroom structure
  • discoveryPublic rankings across Skool

Two ways to run it

On your own machine. Open source, MIT licensed. Your Skool session never leaves your computer. 

pip install git+https://github.com/nklsschroeer707/catknows
claude mcp add catknows -- python -m catknows.mcp_server

Hosted. One endpoint, nothing to install. You connect Skool through a browser that runs here and is streamed to your screen — your password goes to Skool, never to us. Google and Apple sign-in work for the same reason.

One Skool session per account, encrypted at rest: your data is answered from your session and no one else's. Accounts are created on request.

Connect Skool Already have an account? That link signs you in. Otherwise ask for one.

What it does with your data

Community data is not stored. It is fetched for your request, passed to your AI client, and gone. What persists is your account and your encrypted Skool session — both deletable by you, at any time.

Worth knowing before you connect it. Whatever a tool returns lands in your conversation with your AI provider, and that data includes your members' personal information. Pull what you need, not everything you can.
